TAKE IT WITH YOU

The party ends.
The artifacts don’t.

The best ideas from the planning weren’t venues. They were objects and rituals Laura can keep: tactile, imperfect, and made by the people who love her.

01

One-time-use cameras

One per 2–3 guests, plus two extras. Label them with prompts: Laura laughing, Paris after dark, something she didn’t see. No reviewing during the party.

After: high-res scans + negatives returned
02

The giant birthday card

Turn the oversized plastic card in the living room into a collective artwork: illustration on the front, memories and predictions inside, instant photos added as the weekend unfolds.

Ask for: a memory, an admiration, a prediction
03

Laura’s Paris map

Guests decorate small locks with paint pens, then choose a meaningful permitted or symbolic placement. Photograph each moment, save the coordinates, and explain why the spot matters.

Final form: a printed atlas, not bridge clutter
04

27 reasons we love Laura

Collect one line from every guest. Fold the notes into a beautiful box or scatter them through the weekend as secret messages at each stop.

Small effort, enormous emotional return

THE ENCORE

Six more reasons
to stay in Paris.

Conversation finds that fit the birthday mood—fashion, art, film, food, and a little more mischief.

01

Vintage treasure hunt

Passion Luxe, Closet 2 Closet, then Rue de Turenne for a bargain Céline or another beautiful accident.

02

Independent Marais

SimpleRainbow at 21 Rue Pastourelle, then wander Rue Charlot and Rue de Saintonge.

03

Palais de Tokyo

A 90-minute art detour: prioritize Jesse Darling, Benoît Piéron, then Cathy de Monchaux.

04

Film-lab finale

Drop the cameras at L’Atelier Publimod in the Marais; request high-resolution scans and returned negatives.

05

Birthday dessert

A simple fruit tart is the elegant move when butter is fine but milk and cream are not. Confirm with the pâtisserie.

06

Progressive Paris night

One meeting point, then reveal each chapter: rooftop champagne, intimate dinner, surprise music, hidden dancing.
